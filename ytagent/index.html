The YouTube agent skill: 11 free Claude skills for your channel

Scripts with a scored hook, title and thumbnail checked as one pair, dead air cut from your transcript, the Shorts hiding in your long videos, a week that fits your real hours, and a retention reader that shows the exact second people left and what you were saying.

GitHub: Jakeschincariol/youtube-agent-skillMIT licenseNo signup · no API key

Repo: github.com/Jakeschincariol/youtube-agent-skill

1Paste the link into Claude

In Claude Code (or any Claude that can install skills), paste this:

https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/youtube-agent-skill

Install this skill, then confirm /yt-script works.

Prefer to do it yourself? Either as a plugin:

/plugin marketplace add Jakeschincariol/youtube-agent-skill
/plugin install youtube-agent

or by copying the folders:

git clone https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/youtube-agent-skill.git
cp -r youtube-agent-skill/skills/yt-* ~/.claude/skills/

2Spend ten minutes on your voice file

Copy templates/voice.md to ~/.claude/youtube/voice.md and fill it in, or send Claude three of your own videos and say "write my voice.md from these". Every skill reads it, and on YouTube you say the words out loud, so it matters.

3Start with these three

/yt-retentionExport your retention chart (Studio → a video → Analytics → Engagement → audience retention → download) and hand it over, ideally with the transcript. You get the hook leak, the cliffs, the slide, and one change for the biggest leak.
/yt-scriptOne idea in, five hooks off 21 formulas, each scored on specificity, address, stakes, curiosity and brevity, then the spoken script with retention beats marked.
/yt-packageTitle and thumbnail linted as one pairing: truncation at ~60 characters on desktop and ~40 on mobile, duplicated words, too many words on the thumbnail.

All eleven

  • /yt-script one idea into a script with a scored hook
  • /yt-package title and thumbnail as one pairing
  • /yt-edit a transcript into an edit decision list: dead air, filler, retakes, with timecodes
  • /yt-comment comments triaged into four piles, replies in your voice, which one to pin
  • /yt-plan a week that fits your hours: one anchor, one cheap one, three Shorts
  • /yt-viral what works in your niche, ranked by how far each video beat its own channel
  • /yt-retention where viewers actually leave and what to change
  • /yt-shorts the Shorts already inside a long video, with a new first line for each
  • /yt-seo description, the tags worth having, the three queries it should win
  • /yt-chapters chapters validated against YouTube's own rules
  • /yt-audit the whole channel, ending in one fix rather than twenty

Honest notes

  • It does not publish. The skills write, you upload. Every run ends with "ship it, or change it?"
  • The hook score is a heuristic calibrated on 74 real hooks, not a view predictor. A low score means look again; a high one is no promise.
  • Six small Python tools do the scoring and parsing (plain Python 3, no dependencies). Without Claude Code you can paste a single SKILL.md into a chat, but you lose those tools.
  • /yt-viral only reads public listings and never logs in as you.
  • The example hooks, titles and timecodes in the video are illustrations; the scores shown came from running the repo's own hookscore.py and title.py on them.
  • Free and open source under MIT.
