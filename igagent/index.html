The Instagram agent skill: your Reels, captions and week, written by Claude

Thirteen free Claude skills for one Instagram account. They write your Reels off 26 hook formulas and score the hook before you film, find the reels that are working in your niche, lint your caption, draft replies to your comments and plan your week. You still hit post yourself.

Repo: github.com/Jakeschincariol/instagram-agent-skill

1Install it

Easiest: paste this into Claude and send it.

https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/instagram-agent-skill

Install this skill, then confirm /ig-reel works.

Or yourself, in Claude Code (terminal):

git clone https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/instagram-agent-skill.git
cp -r instagram-agent-skill/skills/ig-* ~/.claude/skills/

Or as a plugin, inside Claude Code:

/plugin marketplace add Jakeschincariol/instagram-agent-skill
/plugin install instagram-agent
No Claude Code? Paste any single SKILL.md from the repo at the top of a normal Claude chat and it runs as a mode. You lose the five Python tools (hook scorer, beat sheet, caption linter, humanizer, swipe file), which is most of the point of /ig-reel and /ig-human, but the rest works.

2Spend ten minutes on your voice

Every skill reads one file: ~/.claude/instagram/voice.md. Copy templates/voice.md from the repo there and fill it in, or send Claude three of your own reels and say "write my voice.md from these". Skip it and everything comes out sounding like everyone else.

3Start with these four

/ig-reelOne idea into a Reel: three hooks from 26 formulas, scored, then the script, the on-screen text and a timed beat sheet.
/ig-viralFinds what is working in your niche and ranks it by how far each reel beat its own account's median, not by raw views. Names the hook formula and writes a swipe file. You drive your own browser, it never logs in as you.
/ig-captionShows you the roughly 125 characters the feed shows before "more", then lints length, hook, hashtags (max five), the one call to action and your search terms.
/ig-planThe week: what to post, which format, when, and the 10 accounts to engage with.

The other nine

  • /ig-carousel swipe posts: the cover, slide copy and the 1080x1350 files
  • /ig-story the daily story sequence and the DM funnel
  • /ig-profile scores your profile out of 100 and rewrites what lost points
  • /ig-human the humanizer: strips em dashes, stock phrases and invisible characters, then scores the draft on five checks
  • /ig-comment comments on other people's posts (never "🔥🔥🔥")
  • /ig-reply sorts the comments under your post (keyword, lead, question, …) and drafts replies in that order
  • /ig-dm keyword delivery, first message, collab pitch and two follow-ups
  • /ig-repurpose one video, podcast or newsletter into a week of reels and carousels
  • /ig-audit post-mortem on what you already posted, ranked by outlier multiple and sends per reach

Honest notes

  • It does not post for you. Every skill ends in a copy-ready block. Automating posting, comments or DMs through a browser or third-party tool breaks Instagram's terms and gets accounts blocked, so the repo deliberately does not do it.
  • The hook scorer is good at catching weak hooks (greetings, preambles, nothing concrete). The author measured it: it does not reliably pick which of two good hooks will win. Kill the weak ones, then trust your own retention graph.
  • The humanizer's checks are local heuristics, not GPTZero or similar. Nobody can honestly promise "undetectable".
