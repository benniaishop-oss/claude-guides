Privacy Policy

Benni Online Publisher

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Benni Online Publisher ("the app") is a private publishing tool operated by Benjamin Lischka, Sittendorf, Austria ("we"). It posts short educational videos about Claude Code to our own TikTok and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public and has a single user: us.

What data the app handles

  • Our own account data: the access token TikTok issues for our account, our username and profile basics, and the list of videos we published.
  • Our own content: the video files and captions we upload.
  • Performance data of our own videos: view, like, comment and share counts, used to improve our content.

The app does not collect, store or process data about other TikTok users. It does not read private messages. It does not track anyone.

How the data is used

Solely to upload our videos, to read how they performed, and to keep the app logged in. Nothing else.

Storage and security

Access tokens and logs are stored on our own computer, encrypted at rest by the operating system, and are never shared, sold or transferred to third parties. Video files are hosted temporarily for upload and deleted afterwards.

Third parties

The app talks to TikTok's API (governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy) and to Meta's Instagram API. No advertising or analytics providers are involved.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when we disconnect the app from our TikTok account (TikTok app settings → Security → Apps and services), which we can do at any time. Performance data is kept only as long as it is useful for planning content.
