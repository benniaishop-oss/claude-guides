Life Automation Skills: Claude does the admin of your week

Thirteen free Claude skills for the repetitive part of a working week: your inbox, meetings, follow-ups, receipts and unpaid invoices. They all read one shared profile, so they know your priorities, your clients and your tone. Everything lands as a draft. You always hit send.

1Install it

Easiest: paste this into Claude and send it.

https://github.com/charlie947/life-automation-skills

Install these skills, then run company-setup.

Or yourself, with the skills CLI:

npx skills add charlie947/life-automation-skills

Only want one? Add it on its own:

npx skills add charlie947/life-automation-skills/inbox-executive-assistant
No terminal? Every skill is a plain SKILL.md. Drop any skills/<name>/ folder into your agent's skills directory and it works.

2Run company-setup once

This is the keystone. It interviews you in a few short rounds and writes company-profile.md: your priorities, VIPs, working hours, tone and tools. Every other skill reads it first, which is why the drafts sound like you and the inbox knows who matters. Keep that file private; it should never go into a public repo.

3Connect what the skills need

The skills use Claude's normal connectors, login only, no code:

  • Gmail or Microsoft 365 for the inbox, follow-ups, receipts and invoice reminders
  • Google Calendar for meetings and the weekly review
  • Xero or Stripe (read-only) if you want overdue invoices found for you
  • Airtable or a paste-ready sheet block for the expense log

If a connector is missing, the skill tells you which one and stops. It never guesses.

4Start with these five

inbox-executive-assistantTriages your inbox into a prioritised brief, drafts the replies and lists today's meetings. Try: "catch me up on my inbox".
meeting-schedulerFinds free slots, books, reschedules and sends holds.
follow-up-chaserFinds the threads where you are waiting on someone and drafts a polite nudge. Never passive-aggressive.
expense-wranglerPulls receipts out of email into clean rows. If an amount only sits in a PDF it cannot read, it marks the row [NEEDS: open attachment] instead of guessing.
invoice-chaserSpots overdue invoices in Xero or Stripe and drafts the reminder, gentle first, then firm, then final notice, by days overdue.

The rest of the pack

  • meeting-notes summarises a meeting and routes the action items
  • weekly-review turns the week's calendar and inbox into a plan
  • task-capture pulls tasks out of email and chat into your list
  • research-brief a cited brief on any topic
  • standup-writer your status update from what you actually did
  • doc-drafter a first draft of a doc or contract from a brief
  • crm-hygiene dedupes and tidies your contact records

Honest notes

  • Nothing sends on its own. Replies, nudges and reminders land in Drafts. Deletes and other changes are proposed and only done on your yes.
  • It is on-demand help, not a background robot. You ask, it drafts.
  • Emails are treated as untrusted: the skills never follow instructions hidden inside a message and never open links from it.
  • It never invents money. Amounts come from your accounting data or stay blank for you to fill.
  • Free and open source under MIT. You bring your own accounts.
