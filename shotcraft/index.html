video-shotcraft: Claude makes the launch video for your product

Point Claude at your app and it captures your real pages, writes a storyboard shot by shot, moves the camera through your screens, cuts every shot on the beat and adds the sound design. When it's done, you fine-tune the film yourself in a browser editor.

GitHub: Vincentwei1021/video-shotcraftApache-2.0Claude Code · Codex

Repo: github.com/Vincentwei1021/video-shotcraft · all shots with live previews: the Gallery

1Paste the link into Claude

In Claude Code, paste this (it's the exact line the README recommends):

Install this skill for me: https://github.com/Vincentwei1021/video-shotcraft

Claude clones the repo and links it into your skills folder. Prefer to do it yourself? With the skills CLI:

npx skills add Vincentwei1021/video-shotcraft

or by hand:

git clone https://github.com/Vincentwei1021/video-shotcraft.git
cd video-shotcraft
ln -s "$(pwd)" ~/.claude/skills/video-shotcraft
You need Node.js. The videos are rendered with Remotion, a React video framework, so Claude will install its packages the first time you render.

2Ask for your first film

The fastest path is the built-in template Ink Press: a tested 36.2-second promo, 1920×1080 at 30 fps, 10 shots with camera moves, title cards, transitions and a full sound pass. Claude swaps in your screenshots, copy and branding.

Use video-shotcraft to make a promo for my product with the Ink Press template.

Or pick shots yourself in the Gallery and name them:

Use the deck-deal-flyin and row-embed shot cards to present this feature.

If you don't name a shot, the skill shows you the template first and asks whether to use it.

3Edit the result in the workbench

After delivery the skill opens the Motion Workbench in your browser, a CapCut-style timeline with shot, transition, caption and sound tracks. Click any shot to change its text, font sizes and colours, trim or speed-ramp clips, drag in any of the 216 demo motions, then export again. Preview and render match frame for frame.

node workbench/scripts/open.mjs <project>

What's inside

157 shot recipe cardsPurpose, energy, suggested duration, parameters and known pitfalls for each shot, with 214 motion previews in the Gallery. Many were studied from official product films by Notion, Slack, Figma, Framer, Raycast, Loom and others, then re-built from scratch (no footage from those films is included).
Real page capturesScripts that capture your actual product pages, then 2.5D camera moves glide through them.
Beat syncThe music is analysed and cuts land on the beat.
149 sound effectsSorted into 16 categories (transition, impact, riser, camera, ui, text, paper, film, light, data, scifi, mech, glass, fluid, crowd, counter), plus 5 music beds.

Honest notes

  • The skill is free (Apache-2.0). Remotion, which renders the video, has its own license: free for individuals and small teams, larger companies may need a paid Remotion license.
  • It's built for web and desktop product promos (landscape 1920×1080). Single shot cards also work for feature demos and launch clips.
  • The template ships with demo screenshots. Replace them with your own and check that no customer or personal data is visible before you publish.
  • Rendering takes real computer time. On a small headless Linux box the README lists three flags you may need (--concurrency=1, a chrome-headless-shell binary, --browser-executable).
  • The export to JianYing (CapCut China) is tested on macOS only.
  • The dashboard, captions and numbers in the Instagram video are illustrations; the counts (157 recipe cards, 149 sound effects, 9,629 stars on 26 Sep 2026) come from the repo.
