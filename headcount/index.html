Headcount: a whole company inside Claude Code

172 free skills, organised like a company: a chief executive over 16 departments. Finance, marketing, revenue, legal, security, people and more. You never pick the expert. You ask your question and the right specialist loads itself.

1Add the marketplace

Open Claude Code and paste this once:

/plugin marketplace add cbrock84/headcount

2Install the departments you need

Every department is its own plugin. Install one at a time, for example:

/plugin install finance@headcount
/plugin install marketing@headcount
/plugin install legal-risk@headcount
Install what you will use, not everything. A smaller set triggers more precisely. You can add more at any time, and /plugin on its own opens the menu to review or remove them.

3Just ask

Skills load themselves when a request matches. Examples straight from the README:

"why isn't this landing page converting?"loads demand-generation:landing-page-cro-expert
"can we afford this hire?"loads finance:unit-economics
"our growth has stalled"loads executive:business-growth-consultant
"review this design before we build it"loads security:threat-modeling

Want a specific lens? Call it by name, e.g. /finance:cost-accounting.

Which departments to take first

  • Run a small company: executive, finance, operations
  • Own sales or growth: revenue, marketing, demand-generation
  • Build a product: product, technology, data-analytics
  • Hire people: people, legal-risk
  • Always add early: security and legal-risk. They are the two that tell you what you did not want to hear.

Skills from the video

  • demand-generation:landing-page-cro-expert finds why a page is not converting and rewrites headline, hero copy and calls to action
  • finance:unit-economics margin, payback and lifetime value: does each customer actually make you money
  • revenue:deal-negotiation prepares your walk-away and theirs, trades concessions instead of giving them away
  • marketing:product-launch tiers the launch and sequences internal readiness before the announcement
  • executive:saas-idea-validator scores problem, market, competition, monetization, defensibility and execution, and returns a verdict rather than encouragement

Honest notes

  • Security and Legal are reviewer-class. Their blocking findings cannot be overruled by the department under review.
  • Installing a department adds skills only. It does not change your project, add dependencies or run anything on its own.
  • These are expert playbooks, not a licensed accountant or lawyer. Skills that touch tax, law or regulation cite their sources (184 so far) so you can check them.
  • Also works in ChatGPT and Codex. There, skill descriptions share a small budget, so installing only a few departments is the only thing that works.
  • Free and open source under MIT.
